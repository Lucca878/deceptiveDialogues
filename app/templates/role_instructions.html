You are the interviewer.

You will have a text conversation with another human participant, the interviewee. Your task is to decide whether their claim about visiting a city is true.

How each conversation works

  1. Read the claim. Each conversation begins automatically with a claim about your partner visiting a city, for example:

  2. Ask 10 questions. You and your partner take turns: one question from you, followed by one answer from your partner. When you have finished your question, click Question complete so your partner can answer.

How each conversation works

  1. Read the claim. Each conversation begins automatically with a claim about visiting a city, for example:

  3. After your tenth answer, the conversation will end and a new conversation (using a new claim) with the same partner will begin.
