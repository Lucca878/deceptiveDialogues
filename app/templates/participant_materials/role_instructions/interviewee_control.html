You are the interviewee.

You will have a text conversation with another human participant, the interviewer. Your task is to convince them that your claim about visiting a city is true.

How each conversation works

  1. Read the claim. Each conversation begins automatically with a claim about visiting a city, for example:
    “I have been to Liverpool in the past three years.”

    You do not need to type the opening claim. It is selected using your earlier answers: a truthful claim concerns a city you reported visiting; a deceptive claim concerns a city you reported not visiting in the past three years.

  2. Answer 10 questions. You and your partner take turns: one question from the interviewer, followed by one answer from you. Regardless of whether the claim is true or false, your goal is to convince the interviewer that the claim is true.
  3. After your tenth answer, the conversation will end and a new conversation (using a new claim) with the same partner will begin.
